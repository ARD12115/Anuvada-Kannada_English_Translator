# Roadmap — Stages 1 to 3

**Core theme (Rule 11):** Language accessibility for everyday digital and civic life in Karnataka. Confirm this framing with your mentor and Department Coordinators before Stage 1 submission.

| | Stage 1 (13 Nov) | Stage 2 (18 Nov) | Stage 3 (26 Nov) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statement | Kannada-English Text Translation Assistant (Theme 3) | AI-Based Spam and Fraud Message Identification (Cybersecurity & Digital Safety) | Intelligent Document Understanding and Automated Information Extraction (Generative AI & Intelligent Systems) |
| Output | Translation from text, voice, signs, notices | Classify messages (scam / safe) in Kannada, Kanglish and English; translate and explain the warning | Upload a Kannada notice/form; extract fields; answer questions in either language |
| Tech | Engines, OCR, glossary | + text classifier, Kanglish normalizer, labeled message set, test results | + LLM/RAG, OCR pipeline, structured extraction, validation results |
| Reuse | New | Stage 1 translator + detector are building blocks | Stage 1 OCR + translation feed document pipeline; Stage 2 detector flags fake notices |

## Rule 12 (progressive improvement)
Depth: API/model use -> trained classifier -> RAG + extraction. Integration: single tool -> safety pipeline -> document platform. Real-world use: understanding -> protection -> automation of civic paperwork.

## Alternative Stage 2 (stronger theme link, weaker "digital safety" story)
Smart City & Urban Development -> Citizen Complaint Prioritization and Resolution Tracking: citizens file complaints in Kannada or English, the system translates, categorizes and prioritizes them. It leads naturally to Stage 3 "Intelligent Document Understanding" or FinTech & Digital Governance -> Smart Digital Governance and Automated Public Grievance Redressal.
